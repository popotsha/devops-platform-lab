Clear storefront cookies

Like kit v2/v3, this storefront uses prefixed Auth.js cookies (authjs.storefront.*). On local Docker, Keycloak cookies on localhost can still accumulate and cause HTTP 431.

  1. Click the button below (clears readable cookies + storage).
  2. If logout/checkout still fail, clear site data for localhost in DevTools → Application (httpOnly cookies cannot be removed from JavaScript).
  3. Return to the storefront and sign in again.
